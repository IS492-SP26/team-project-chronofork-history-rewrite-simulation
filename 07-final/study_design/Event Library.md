**Chinese History**

| **Event Name**           | **Core Background & Decision Logic**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **🔥 Battle of Red Cliffs** | After unifying the north, Cao Cao led his forces southward. Despite internal calls to surrender, Sun Quan allied with Liu Bei and launched a surprise fire attack on Cao Cao's army at Red Cliffs, forcing Cao Cao to retreat north. This battle established the political landscape of the Three Kingdoms. **Hypothetical:** If Sun Quan had chosen to surrender under pressure, would Chinese history have moved toward reunification? |
| **🚢 Opium War**           | In the 19th century, British opium smuggling prompted the Qing court to impose strict prohibition. After Lin Zexu's crackdown on opium, British forces retaliated militarily. The Qing dynasty, outmatched in weapons and systems, was defeated and forced to sign unequal treaties, passively drawn into globalization. **Hypothetical:** If the Qing court had adopted a more flexible diplomatic strategy after the opium ban, could it have avoided having its borders forcibly opened? |
| **📜 Hundred Days' Reform** | After defeat in the First Sino-Japanese War, Emperor Guangxu employed reformists to attempt comprehensive reforms. However, the reform was rushed and lacked military support, ultimately suppressed by Empress Dowager Cixi's coup, causing China to lose an opportunity for peaceful transformation. **Hypothetical:** If the reform had taken a gradual and steady approach, could it have effectively weakened the resistance of the conservative faction? |

**Global History**

| **Event Name**             | **Core Background & Decision Logic**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **🚢 Titanic**              | In 1912, this massive ship ignored iceberg warnings, sailed at excessive speed, and had insufficient lifeboat capacity, resulting in a collision with an iceberg on its maiden voyage. The disaster exposed technological hubris and class inequality, triggering global maritime safety reforms. **Hypothetical:** If navigation orders had called for reducing speed and diverting earlier, could this civilizational tragedy have been avoided? |
| **☢️ Cuban Missile Crisis** | In 1962, the US and USSR engaged in an extreme standoff over nuclear missile deployment in Cuba. Kennedy faced a difficult choice between air strikes and a blockade, ultimately resolving the crisis through secret diplomacy and establishing the Cold War's crisis management model. **Hypothetical:** If Kennedy had followed the military's advice to conduct air strikes, could humanity have escaped nuclear war? |
| **🛩️ Pearl Harbor**         | Japan launched a surprise attack to eliminate American interference. Though the attack severely damaged the US fleet, it completely awakened America's war potential, leading to full US entry into the war and accelerating the defeat of the Axis powers. **Hypothetical:** If Japan had avoided provoking the US military, would American isolationism have caused the US to delay entering the war? |
